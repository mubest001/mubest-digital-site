WHAT YOU GET
- Blue + lime accents, neutral backgrounds and text across public pages.
- 3 video hero slides: editorial, split marketing, centered ecommerce.
- 8-second auto advance, previous/next arrows, progress indicators, touch swipe,
  arrow-key navigation, pause on hover/focus/hidden tab, reduced-motion support.
- One active video at a time; the other two do not preload until active.
- Videos autoplay muted (browser policies may still block playback); CSS fallback remains.

EDITING THE SLIDES VIA YOUR EXISTING DASHBOARD
Slide 1 content: Admin > Pages > Home > Hero section.
Slide 1 video/poster: Admin > Homepage & hero > Video carousel · slide 1.
Slides 2 & 3 content and MP4/poster URLs: Admin > Pages > Home > Video carousel slide 2 / slide 3.

To upload your own hero videos, add them to Media library, copy their public URLs,
paste into the slide video fields, then publish the Home page. The site reads Neon
at runtime, so these content changes will not need a new Vercel deployment.
